Part 1 — What Happened

On 19 August 2026, Moderna and MSD announced the first positive Phase 3 readout for a personalised mRNA cancer therapy. The INTerpath-001 trial (NCT05933577, 1,137 patients, resected high-risk melanoma) met both its primary endpoint (recurrence-free survival) and key secondary endpoint (distant metastasis-free survival), with a 59% reduction in the risk of distant metastasis or death compared to KEYTRUDA alone.

This is the first Phase 3 proof-of-principle that personalised mRNA cancer vaccines work — and it validates the entire platform, including BioNTech's parallel pipeline (autogene cevumeran; 15 Phase 3 oncology trials planned by year-end 2026; €17.2B cash committed).
