01 · THE QUESTION

Is the grid constraint a theme or a moment?

Every investor looking at the compute buildout now asks the same thing: the electricity problem is obviously real, but is it a durable allocation theme or a news cycle that has already peaked?

Attention data can answer that, on one condition — that the measuring instrument has not moved. In September 2026 we established that it had. Every figure in this report is corrected for it first, because without the correction the answer comes out wrong in a specific and flattering direction.

02 · THE CORRECTION, FIRST

The ruler moved in August 2025

Five semantically inert control terms — iPhone, pizza recipe, banana bread, washing machine repair, driving licence — rise together from August 2025 by a median factor of 1.31. Nothing plausible moves those five in concert. Google Trends reports share of search, not volume, and the share base is contracting.

CONTROL BASKET · 5 INERT TERMS · INDEXED, 100 = 2023–24 AVERAGE
control basket2023–24 basestructural break

The basket holds flat around 100 for thirty-one months, then steps up and stays up. It sits at 148 today. That step is not the world becoming more interested in banana bread: it is the denominator shrinking underneath every Google Trends series simultaneously.

Consequence for this report. A term sitting at 19 before the break crosses the 25 threshold after it without anything happening in the world. Every month from August 2025 onward is divided by 1.31 before being compared with the threshold; earlier months are left untouched, because the drift is dated. In this cluster the correction changes one classification — grid interconnection queue reaches twelve months uncorrected and six once deflated — and it lowers every current reading by roughly a quarter.

Independent cross-check, on a sensor that counts volume rather than share: total English Wikipedia traffic fell 7.0% year on year over the same window. One measure of share rising while the measure of volume falls is the signature of a denominator effect, not of demand.

03 · THE FINDING

Narrative vocabulary spiked. Procurement vocabulary held.

Zorthex classifies a phenomenon as structural when it holds at or above the threshold for twelve consecutive months. Applied to the eight terms of the grid-and-compute cluster, deflated, the cluster splits cleanly in two — and the split is not where the coverage would put it.

The terms that describe how power is actually bought and connected — behind the meter, microgrid, onsite power — crossed the threshold and are still above it, fourteen months later and counting. The terms the trade press invented to describe the same story — energy certainty, utility sovereignty — reached seven months and fell back below.

The phrase did its work and is being retired. The activity it named has not stopped.

04 · THE OTHER SOURCES

What the volume and community measures say

Wikipedia — the volume measure

Search share tells you what people are asking. It does not tell you whether the subject is consolidating. For that, Zorthex reads a sensor that counts rather than ranks: Wikipedia pageviews, normalised for the 7.0% systemic decline.

The Data center article rises sharply — but by how much depends entirely on what it is measured against, and that choice deserves to be shown rather than buried.

Normalising a technical article against site-wide Wikipedia traffic is too coarse. The 7.0% site-wide decline is not uniform: descriptive articles about physical infrastructure are more exposed to the migration of informational queries than the average page. So the article was also measured against a matched basket — five descriptive articles about physical infrastructure, outside computing, chosen to share its function and its exposure rather than its subject: Sewage treatment, Blast furnace, Wind turbine, Oil refinery, Desalination. That basket fell by a median of 27.6% over the same window, four times the site-wide rate.

Three denominators, one direction. The direction is robust; the magnitude is not, and this report claims only the first. Note which way the correction runs: the more rigorous normaliser produces the larger figure, which means the site-wide estimate was understating the signal rather than flattering it.

The matched basket carries its own limits and they are not small. Five articles is a thin sample; the dispersion runs from −38.0% to +13.7%, and Desalination at +13.7% plausibly has a cycle of its own. The selection of controls is a judgement, and a different basket would give a different number. It is offered as a second reading, not as a replacement.

Reddit — the community measure

The third source is qualitative by design and is never converted into a number or used to compute L. It answers a different question: is anyone actually discussing this, and who? A phenomenon with high search interest and no community may be a media artefact; one with low search interest and an active community may be early.

Sampled through Reddit's public search feed, which returns the most recent matches rather than a count, the cluster reads as follows.

The third source confirms the finding from an angle the first two cannot reach. The procurement terms are not merely searched: they are discussed, in real technical and investor communities, continuously. The two narrative terms have no community at all — it is not that attention faded, it is that a discussion never formed around them. A phrase can generate search interest for seven months without a single durable thread behind it, and that is what these two did.

One term must be flagged. On Reddit, stranded silicon appears almost entirely in hobby-electronics communities discussing silicon as a material, not idle compute capacity. The phrase is ambiguous, which means its Google Trends series is probably contaminated by the unrelated sense. It is reported above for completeness and should not be relied on until disambiguated — a check the first two sources could not have produced on their own.

Sensor agreement is the discriminator between a sustained break and an event-driven one. Where the sources diverge, the attention is narrative. Where they converge, something underneath is consolidating. On the procurement terms all three converge; on the narrative terms all three are absent or falling.

04b · SENSITIVITY

Does the finding survive the range of the correction?

The deflation factor is a median of five control terms spanning 1.25 to 1.35. A conclusion that depends on which end of that range is chosen is not a conclusion. Every term was therefore recomputed at both extremes.

Six of eight terms hold their classification across the whole range. No current value crosses the threshold as a function of the factor: 29.0 stays above at every factor, 22.9 stays below at every factor. The grey zone one might expect around the threshold does not materialise here.

The two terms that are sensitive are not the ones carrying the conclusion. data center power is already described above as a run that has ended and retraced; grid interconnection queue is already excluded for sparse history. The three procurement terms hold fourteen months at every factor in the range, and the two narrative terms stay below at every factor. The finding is robust to the correction it depends on.

05 · THE READ

Where the window is, how wide, what type

Where

Not in "AI infrastructure", which is saturated and carries no information. The open window is one layer below, in the language of power procurement: behind-the-meter generation, microgrids, onsite generation. That vocabulary crossed the threshold and is holding.

How wide

Fourteen months and open. Three terms remain above the deflated threshold with no sign of retreat, against a cluster average that has already turned down. In this dataset a structural run that is still extending at fourteen months has historically had further to go, though that is a base rate and not a forecast.

What type

Sustained, not event-driven — on the evidence of sensor convergence, not of magnitude. The distinction matters because the magnitude is exactly what the baseline drift inflates, and what a reader without the correction would have used to reach the opposite conclusion about the retreating terms.

The diagnostic statement. The attention window in energy-for-compute is open in procurement vocabulary and closing in narrative vocabulary. A reader tracking the phrases that appear in headlines is tracking the half of this theme that has already peaked.

06 · LIMITATIONS

What this report does not claim

  • Zorthex is diagnostic, not predictive. It describes where an attention window is open and of what kind. It contains no price, no security, and no recommendation, and it has no established linkage to returns.
  • The deflation factor of 1.31 is a median across five control terms with a range of 1.25 to 1.35. Section 04b reports every figure recomputed at both extremes; two terms are sensitive to the choice and are identified there.
  • Several of these terms are industry coinages with low absolute search volume. Low-volume series are subject to Google Trends quantisation and are noisier than mass-market terms.
  • grid interconnection queue is reported for completeness but its early history is too sparse to normalise reliably; it is excluded from the cluster read.
  • stranded silicon is ambiguous — the community source shows the phrase is dominated by an unrelated hobby-electronics sense — and its Google Trends series should be treated as contaminated pending disambiguation.
  • The eight terms measured here are the English-language procurement vocabulary of this theme, which is predominantly the North American register. The same phenomenon is named differently elsewhere: European coverage of the identical problem uses regulatory language — grid saturation, connection agreements, waste heat reuse — while siting and policy discussions use other vocabularies again. This report measures one register and does not claim to cover the theme.
  • The Reddit reading is a qualitative assessment on a declared three-level scale, sampled through a public feed that returns recent matches rather than counts. It is never converted into a number and never used to compute L. It confirms, contradicts or qualifies; it does not measure.
  • The Wikipedia figures are year-on-year comparisons, not monthly series. Three normalisers are reported because they disagree on magnitude while agreeing on direction; only the direction is claimed. The matched basket has n=5 and a dispersion of −38.0% to +13.7%, and the choice of controls is discretionary.
  • The threshold rule of twelve consecutive months is a convention of the Zorthex method, declared in the methodology and applied identically here. It is not derived from this data.
