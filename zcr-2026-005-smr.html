// 01 · t_start definition

When Did SMR Begin?

t_start selected: May 2005
Source: IAEA TECDOC-1451 — "Innovative Small and Medium Sized Reactors: Design Features, Safety Approaches and R&D Trends" · Level A (international institutional publication)

Rationale: First institutional document formalising SMR as a reactor category with defined parameters (≤300 MWe, modular design, factory-fabricated). This moment marks when SMR transitioned from individual research projects to a recognised institutional category with international standards.

2000–2004

DOE Nuclear Energy Research Initiative funds early SMR concepts. Oregon State University research (basis for NuScale). Conceptual phase — no formalised category exists.

May 2005 ★

IAEA TECDOC-1451 published. First international institutional framework defining SMR as a category. This is t_start. Level A.

Nov 2007

NuScale Power founded. First company exclusively focused on SMR commercialisation. Level B.

Mar 2008

First Google Trends signal: 3/100. The term enters measurable search behaviour.

Mar 2012

DOE SMR Licensing Technical Support program announced. $452M over five years. Level A.

Jan 2023

NuScale receives NRC Standard Design Approval — the only NRC-certified SMR design. Level A.

Oct 2024

Amazon anchors $500M investment in X-energy. Google, Microsoft announce nuclear PPAs. Google Trends rises to 13/100.

Jun 2025

TerraPower closes $650M Series C (Bill Gates, NVIDIA NVentures). X-energy raises $700M. $1.3B total equity in 2025.

Aug 2025 ★

Public attention breakout. Google Trends: 16 → 100 in one month. t_peak confirmed.

Apr 2026

X-energy IPO: $1.02 billion. First pure-play SMR IPO. Holtec files S-1 targeting $10B+ valuation.

Jul 2026

Deployable Energy Unity reaches criticality — third microreactor under DOE July 4 directive. Bank of America estimates $10 trillion nuclear opportunity. Last month above threshold (44/100).

Aug 2026 ⚡

Score drops to 23/100 — below threshold. First month below since breakout. 12 consecutive months completed exactly. STRUCTURAL classification locked. Operational status: Receding.

// 04 · why the L value matters

243 Months of Invisible Infrastructure

For 20 years, the IAEA published technical standards, NuScale obtained the only NRC design certification, TerraPower secured $650M from Bill Gates and NVIDIA, X-energy raised $700M from Amazon, and China built the world's first commercial land-based SMR. All of this happened while Google Trends showed 2–7 out of 100.

The breakout in August 2025 closed that window. It was triggered by a convergence of signals: AI data centre energy demand, DOE executive orders mandating three operational reactors by July 4, 2026, and $1.3 billion in equity investment in a single year.

What the Receding status adds: The window opened and closed in exactly 12 months. The public discovered SMR, processed it, and moved on. The attention lasted long enough to qualify as structural (the term entered the vocabulary) but not long enough to sustain active interest. This is the signature of a phenomenon that was catapulted into public awareness by a specific narrative (AI energy demand) rather than by sustained consumer engagement. When the narrative moves on, the attention follows.

For comparison: RWA Tokenization (STRUCTURAL · Active, current 40) and Stablecoins (STRUCTURAL · Active, current ~31) are both above threshold. Their attention is sustained because the underlying activity generates continuous search demand. SMR's attention was driven by investment narrative, not operational demand — nobody Googles "small modular reactor" because they're buying one. The Receding status documents this structural difference.

// 07 · declared limitations

Limitations of This Report

  • t_start alternative candidates: Four alternatives exist: 2001 (DOE conceptualisation, L=295), 2007 (NuScale founding, L=213), 2008 (first GT signal, L=209), 2012 (DOE SMR program, L=161). The 2005 choice reflects first institutional formalisation. Rationale declared, alternatives documented.
  • Query specificity: "Small modular reactor" captures the generic category. Branded queries ("NuScale," "Oklo," "TerraPower") would produce different values. The US geographic score (20) is likely depressed by query fragmentation across brand names.
  • Threshold boundary case: SMR qualified at exactly 12 months — the minimum. No margin. The STRUCTURAL classification is technically correct but operationally fragile. The Receding status documents this fragility. If the rule were 13 months, SMR would not qualify. This is declared, not hidden.
  • August 2026 reading: The August 2026 score (23/100) is from the Zorthex dataset revision, not a new CSV lock. The next CSV lock is scheduled for the November 2026 revision.
  • Wikipedia source: en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_modular_reactor has 684 views/day average. Event-driven spikes make monthly averages volatile.
