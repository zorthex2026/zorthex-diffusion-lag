Post-Quantum Cryptography —
Public Attention Diffusion Lag Analysis
◷ Under Revision

This report is temporarily under revision.

Zorthex measures diffusion lag from Google Trends data, which is normalized against the peak of the selected observation window. As the underlying window extends, earlier values are rescaled — so figures are periodically re-verified against the most recent data before being treated as final.

The diffusion-lag figures for Post-Quantum Cryptography are currently being recalculated against the updated window ahead of the Zorthex v2.0 release. Updated values will be published here shortly.

The phenomenon itself is well documented: the NIST Post-Quantum Cryptography standardization process (competition launch 2017; FIPS 203/204/205 published August 2024) and the subsequent consolidation of institutional attention. What is being re-verified is the precise dating and classification, not the existence of the transition.
