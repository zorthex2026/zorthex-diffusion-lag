How to Read This Dataset

Every case has two axes: classification (what happened — permanent) and operational status (what's happening now — updated every 90 days).

Classification — What happened
Did this phenomenon enter the public vocabulary? Based on 12 consecutive months ≥25/100 on Google Trends. Once earned, permanent — like a credit rating at issuance.
STR
STRUCTURAL

12+ consecutive months above threshold. The term entered the public vocabulary. Permanent classification.

OBS
OBSERVATION

Above threshold but fewer than 12 months. The window is open — consolidation not yet confirmed.

S&R
SPIKE & RETREAT

Reached threshold but did not sustain 12 months. Breakout occurred, consolidation did not.

BUB
BUBBLE

Short, sharp spike that collapsed. Never sustained. The term was noise, not signal.

PRE
PRE / NON-STAT

Peak precedes Google Trends data (pre-2004) or confounded by external shock (COVID).

Operational Status — What's happening now
Is the attention alive, fading, or gone? Applied only to STRUCTURAL cases. Updated every 90 days — the current weather, not the climate.
DOM
Dominant

Above threshold for 40+ consecutive months. Permanent vocabulary — will not return below threshold.

ACT
Active

Above threshold right now. Consolidated and alive. The term is current.

REC
Receding

STRUCTURAL but currently below threshold. The term consolidated once — attention is now fading.

CYC
Cyclical

Moves above and below threshold in waves. Tied to market cycles or recurring events.

DEA
Dead

The term has been replaced or abandoned. Classification is permanent; the word is not.

Google Trends — who searches to act. L is computed here. CSV locked.
Wikipedia — who reads to understand. Pageviews via Wikimedia API.
Reddit — how it's discussed. Qualitative proxy (declared).
L (months) — gap between first emergence and sustained public attention.
Example reading: "RWA Tokenization · L=103 · STRUCTURAL · Active · 40/100" → The technology existed for 103 months before the public noticed. It entered the vocabulary (STRUCTURAL). It's still above threshold right now (Active). Current attention: 40/100.
Contrast: "SMR · L=243 · STRUCTURAL · Receding · 23/100" → Same classification, different story. SMR consolidated (12 months above threshold) but attention has now dropped below. The discovery phase happened; the term is fading from active search. Both are STRUCTURAL — one is alive, one is cooling.
