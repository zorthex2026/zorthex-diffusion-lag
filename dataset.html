How to read L. L = t_peak − t_start, in months — the gap between first documented public emergence and sustained mainstream attention. L is stable when the all-time peak is historical; cases whose peak is recent carry a provisional L and are marked OBSERVATION. Where the third factor reveals a B2B / policy-driven phenomenon (operational attention high, informational low), this dual-velocity pattern is declared, not corrected. Spike & Retreat (new, August 2026): cases where attention reached the ≥25/100 threshold but did not sustain for 12 consecutive months — breakout occurred, consolidation did not. The internal dataset extends beyond these public cases; pre-dataset and non-stationary cases are retained for completeness but excluded from L averaging.

Dataset records (August 2026): Longest consecutive run: Streaming (218 months, 18 years). Longest lag: Digital Nomad (L=325 months). Longest financial run: BNPL (89 months). Highest active current: mRNA Cancer Vaccines (100/100). Most dead: ICO Boom (current 3). First PRE→peak: mRNA Cancer Vaccines.
